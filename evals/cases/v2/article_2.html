Visiting the Gower coast

Croeso i Benrhyn Gŵyr! Visitors are welcomed in Welsh at every visitor centre.

Visitor numbers have grown each year since 2023.

Getting to the start of the walk

  1. Park at the Rhossili village car park.
  2. Walk past the visitor centre to the gate.
  3. Follow the cliff path west to the headland.
